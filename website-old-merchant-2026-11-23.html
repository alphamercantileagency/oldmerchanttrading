We supply the packaging a producer’s work deserves: coffee bags, pouches, and tins made to be picked up, kept, and remembered — artisanal in look, industrial in process, priced from the arithmetic up so a small house can sell in beautiful packaging and keep its margin.

Old Merchant is a business-to-business house. We do not sell shelf novelties; we build the container around the product — the roast, the blend, the harvest — and around the producer’s numbers, so beauty is a costed line in a worked stack, never an accident.

Tins
